# Weave Destination Experience

An interactive travel discovery experience designed for exhibition visitors.

Visitors enter their name, select a region, and receive a randomly generated destination card that can be saved or shared. The project explores how simplicity, surprise, and visual storytelling can transform destination discovery into a memorable experience.

⸻

## Overview

Travel discovery today is often driven by search results, rankings, and information overload.

Weave Destination Experience proposes a different approach.

Instead of searching for destinations, visitors discover destinations through chance. By combining a lightweight interaction with a personalized result card, the experience encourages curiosity, exploration, and emotional engagement.

Originally developed as an exhibition experience, the project focuses on creating memorable moments rather than practical trip planning.

⸻

## Design Goals

- Encourage exploration through curiosity and surprise
- Reduce information overload and decision fatigue
- Create emotional engagement through personalized experiences
- Design an interaction that is simple, memorable, and shareable
